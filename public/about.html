About me

Curious by nature, collaborative by choice.

I'm a second-year engineering student who loves turning messy real-world problems into clean, working solutions.

Python is my go-to tool, whether I'm automating a lab report, analysing sensor data or prototyping a control system. But the part I enjoy most is the people: the best ideas I've had came from teams where everyone felt comfortable speaking up. I like to listen first, connect the dots and keep things moving.

Outside of class I run workshops for first-years, tinker with microcontrollers, and chase the next hackathon.
