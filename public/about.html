About

My story

Engineering with care for the people who rely on it

Python is where I start, whether I'm automating a lab report, analysing sensor data or prototyping a control system. Electrical engineering gives me the hardware side: circuits, sensors and the systems that make ideas physical.

The part I enjoy most is the people. My best ideas have come from teams where everyone felt comfortable speaking up, so I listen first, connect the dots and keep things moving.

Outside class I run Python help sessions for first-years, tinker with microcontrollers and chase the next hackathon.
